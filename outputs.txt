CUDA Lab 2 - Profiling Outputs

This file will be filled with Jetson results by running:
    bash run_lab2.sh

The script capturs the device inventory, vector_add CUDA-event timing,
stride-1/stride-32 timings, compiler ptxas lines a tegrastats sample,
the two Nsight Systems tables, and per-stride Nsight Compute attempts.
If nsight compute is unsupproted, it record the reason and tries nvprof.

The two required explanatory sentences are appendd automatically
at the end once the script finishes.
